About Aurelia Bay

A sanctuary crafted for the art of slowing down

Set along a private crescent of white sand, Aurelia Bay blends contemporary architecture with the warmth of island living. Every villa opens to the sea, every meal is sourced from nearby farms and waters, and every member of our team is devoted to making your stay effortless.

Whether you come to celebrate, reconnect or simply do nothing at all, we shape each day around you.
